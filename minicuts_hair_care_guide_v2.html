    Ask Our Stylist to Help Choose See More Style Fixes

    🧒 Choose by Hair Type

    • Straight hair: short, medium or long options can all be matched depending on whether the child wants a neat, soft or trendy look.
    • Wavy / curly hair: boys can try curly top taper or textured curly crop; girls can try layered curly cut or soft curls.
    • Thick hair: taper, layered cut, U-cut, feather cut.
    • Fine hair: short layers, blunt bob, mushroom cut, soft fringe.

    💙 Choose by Comfort

    • Dislikes combing: shorter or layered style to reduce knots.
    • Dislikes hair wash: simple cut or dry cut where suitable.
    • Cannot sit long: quick, low-maintenance haircut.
    • Loves styling: braids, color spray, curls, K-pop or Korean-inspired styles.
