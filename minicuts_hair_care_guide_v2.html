    Science Corner
    How Hair Actually Works
    Growth Rate
    Kids' hair grows about 1.25 cm per month — faster than adults!
    Hair Structure
    Each strand is made of keratin protein — the same as fingernails.
    Water Fact
    Wet hair can stretch up to 30% before breaking — be gentle!
    UAE Climate
    Dubai heat + AC cold = hair needs extra moisture year-round.
    Scalp pH
    Kids' scalp is more sensitive than adults' — mild shampoo always.
    Trimming Truth
    Cutting doesn't make hair grow faster — but it removes damage!

    Dubai Parents
    Hair Care in the UAE Climate
    ☀️
    Sun Exposure
    Long outdoor play dries hair quickly. Use leave-in conditioner for curly or thick hair.
    ❄️
    Air Conditioning
    Indoor AC removes moisture. Kids with fine hair may feel more static and dryness.
    🏊
    Pool Season
    Chlorine weakens hair. Rinse with clean water before and after pool swimming.
    🌬️
    Dust & Sand
    Desert dust can clog scalp pores. Gentle weekly deep-rinse helps keep scalp clean.

    Bonus Science
    The Science Behind Healthy Hair
    💧
    Moisture = Elasticity
    Hydrated hair bends without breaking. Dry hair snaps more easily under combing.
    🧂
    Scalp Sebum is Good
    Natural oils protect the scalp. Over-washing removes them and causes dryness.
    🌡️
    Heat Weakens Bonds
    Hot water and blow dryers weaken keratin bonds. Cool rinse at the end helps seal the cuticle.
    ✂️
    Split Ends Travel Up
    Untreated split ends split further up the shaft. Regular trims stop the damage spreading.
